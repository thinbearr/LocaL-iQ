# Artificial Intelligence Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a broad domain of computer science dedicated to building software systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human cognition, such as visual perception, natural language understanding, reasoning, and decision making.

## Core Branches of AI

The field of AI encompasses several core subfields and paradigms:

1. **Symbolic AI (Rule-Based Systems)**: Employs explicit logic rules and knowledge bases.
2. **Machine Learning**: Systems that learn patterns directly from data without explicit programming. See [[02_Machine_Learning_Fundamentals|ML Fundamentals]] for details.
3. **Deep Learning**: A specialized subset of ML using deep neural networks. See [[03_Deep_Learning_and_Neural_Networks]].

## Modern Paradigm Shift

In recent years, the paradigm has shifted dramatically toward foundation models and [[05_Large_Language_Models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]]. These models enable sophisticated knowledge systems, such as [[06_Retrieval_Augmented_Generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)]], which combine parametric model knowledge with non-parametric external document retrieval.
